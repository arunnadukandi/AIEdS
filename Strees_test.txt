Read it as: single-request latency is your floor. The concurrency level where avg latency roughly doubles (or errors appear) is your practical ceiling — past that point, requests are queuing behind each other rather than running in parallel. Ollama's timeout in llm_client.py / chatbot.py is 120s, so any avg latency approaching that means cadets will start seeing failures, not just slowness.
